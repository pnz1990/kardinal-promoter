    Freeze agents, stop the line¶

    New in v0.12.0

    v0.11.0 and earlier do not have this. The changelog lists every v0.12.0 change.

    A freeze holds promotions until it is lifted, in every Graph, including versions already on their way. An agent freeze holds the versions agents created and lets people's through. A stop-the-line holds every version. The controller enforces it, not the agent: an agent cannot ignore a freeze.

    # Freeze agents in every namespace until lifted
kardinal freeze --agents --reason "INC-4211: agents paused while we investigate"

# Stop the line for everyone in one namespace, for two hours
kardinal freeze --everyone -n payments --reason "payments incident" --for 2h

# List the freezes
kardinal freeze

# Lift one
kardinal unfreeze kardinal-freeze-agents

    What a freeze holds¶

    A held step waits before it starts, or before its next git step, and its message names the freeze, who froze, since when and why, and the two ways on. A step that is waiting for its PR merge or running its health check finishes, as with pause: stopping it would leave a merged change unverified. Auto-merge of a held step's PR is off.

    The step records the freeze in status.frozenBy (its name and since when) and clears it when no freeze holds it any more, or when a pause holds it instead. The Pipeline's status.environments[].frozenBy names the freeze for each environment it holds, and kardinal get pipelines and kardinal status show such an environment as "Frozen by ".

    Freeze Holds
    --agents Versions whose actor stamp says kind agent (Actors)
    --everyone Every version

    --cluster (the default) covers every namespace; -n <namespace> covers one. --for ends the freeze by itself; without it, the freeze lasts until kardinal unfreeze. Freezing the same scope again updates its reason and end.

    A freeze does not refuse what agents write: an agent can still create versions, which then wait. To refuse every write of one agent, suspend its AgentIdentity.

    Let one change through¶

    During a freeze, kardinal override --freeze lets a pipeline through every freeze that holds its namespace, for one environment, one version, or both, for a while (default one hour):

    kardinal override checkout prod --freeze --reason "hotfix for INC-4211" --expires-in 30m

    The exemption is recorded on the freeze with your name and your actor stamp, and when a step goes on under it, the step writes a FreezeExempted AuditEvent for each freeze it goes through, naming who let it through and why. Only someone who may edit the freeze can add an exemption: a namespace's gate overrides cannot break a cluster freeze.

    An exemption counts for at most a day from when the controller first sees it, whatever --expires-in says, as gate overrides are capped (Helm controller.freezeExemptionMaxMinutes, --freeze-exemption-max-minutes; status.exemptionRecords shows each exemption's end). Break-glass is for people: the <release>-actor admission policy refuses an exemption from an agent, and the controller never honours one stamped as an agent's (the freeze's ExemptionIgnored condition says so).

    Who froze, and who lifted it¶

    A freeze's start and its lift are AuditEvents, FreezeStarted and FreezeLifted, naming who and their verified actor: in the freeze's namespace, or the controller's (the watched namespace in namespace mode) for a freeze of several or every namespace, or when the frozen namespace is gone or the controller may not write there. kardinal unfreeze and the UI record who lifts it (spec.freeze.lifted) just before they delete the window, and a finalizer (kardinal.io/freeze-audit) keeps the window until FreezeLifted is written, so a kubectl delete is recorded too, without an actor. While the controller is down, a deleted freeze waits for it. A freeze being deleted (by kardinal unfreeze, the UI or kubectl delete, and kept by the finalizer) is gone for everything else at once: it holds no step, takes no kardinal override --freeze exemption, is not listed by kardinal freeze, the UI or the MCP freezes tool, and is false in a gate's changewindow.<name>. Freezing its scope again meanwhile is refused with freeze <name> is being lifted; try again shortly (up to 2 minutes; the controller must be running). spec.freeze.lifted only names who lifts it: when the delete fails, kardinal unfreeze reports the error and takes the mark back, and the freeze stays in force. A freeze frozen again while an unfreeze is under way is not deleted: the unfreeze says it changed, and you run it again if you still mean to lift it. A second unfreeze of a freeze already being deleted changes nothing, so the record names who lifted it first.

    An audit write never holds a freeze back. A freeze takes effect (its status is written) whether or not its FreezeStarted record could be written; a failed write is retried, with a FreezeAuditFailed Warning Event. A deleted freeze whose FreezeLifted record still cannot be written after 2 minutes is let go without it, with the same Event.

    In the UI¶

    Every view shows a banner for each active freeze: whom it holds and where, who froze (a person or an automation, as in Who shipped a version), since when and until when, the reason, and how many exemptions are in effect. Stopping the line is in the failed colour, an agent freeze in the blocked one, and the words say which. Unfreeze is there for those who may lift it; for everyone else, and while the controls below are off, the banner shows kardinal unfreeze <name>, with the controller's reason as its tooltip. The stage drawer of an environment a freeze holds says so in Why it's waiting, with the step's message: the freeze, and the commands to lift it or let this environment through. On the station map and the fleet board, a station a freeze holds is marked frozen (❄, "Frozen by "), and Needs attention counts it. The top bar's Freeze menu offers Freeze agents… and Stop the line… (which asks you to type stop). The controls need the UI's TokenReview sign-in (a freeze names who froze) and Helm ui.freezeControls: true, which lets the controller write freezes for allowed callers.

    Who may freeze¶

    A freeze is a cluster-scoped ChangeWindow, so freezing takes cluster rights. The chart's <release>-freezer ClusterRole grants them (and the UI's changewindows/freeze action): bind it to your incident commanders.

    In the UI, the changewindows/freeze action in one namespace (the chart's admin role grants it through a RoleBinding) lets a team freeze its own namespace, and change or lift only the freezes it made there. Lifting or changing someone else's freeze of the namespace, such as an incident commander's stop-the-line, takes the action cluster-wide: the UI answers 403.

    kubectl create clusterrolebinding incident-freezers \
  --clusterrole kardinal-promoter-freezer --group incident-commanders

    The <release>-freezes admission policy records who froze (spec.freeze.createdBy) and who made each exemption, as the API server authenticated them; nobody can name someone else.

    Under the hood¶

    kardinal freeze writes a ChangeWindow named kardinal-freeze-<agents|everyone>[-<namespace>]:

    apiVersion: kardinal.io/v1alpha1
kind: ChangeWindow
metadata:
  name: kardinal-freeze-agents
spec:
  type: blackout
  start: "2026-10-14T09:00:00Z"   # no end: until it is removed
  reason: "INC-4211: agents paused while we investigate"
  freeze:
    actors: agents                 # agents | everyone
    namespaces: []                 # exact names or "prefix*"; empty: every namespace
    createdBy: alice@example.com
    exemptions: []

    The ChangeWindow reconciler writes whether it is active and the exemptions in effect to its status at each boundary; the PromotionStep reconciler holds steps on that status. A freeze can also be named in a gate expression like any ChangeWindow (changewindow.isBlocked(...)). The design: 18-actor-policy §9.
