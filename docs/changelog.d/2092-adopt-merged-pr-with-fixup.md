- **A merged PR with a reviewer's fixup on top is adopted after a restart** (#2092). When the controller restarted after opening a PR, and a reviewer pushed a fixup to it and merged it, the step recorded "no changes" and no `PRMerged` AuditEvent, because only a PR whose head was kardinal's commit counted. kardinal now also adopts the merged PR of its own branch whose commits, as the SCM lists them, include a commit the step pushed. This works on GitHub, GitLab, Forgejo, Gitea, Bitbucket Cloud and Data Center, and Azure DevOps, after squash and rebase merges, and once the branch is deleted. A list longer than one page is not used. Such a PR may have been opened by a person and carry commits kardinal did not push, so it is recorded as adopted, not as kardinal's own: the step's message, the `PRMerged` AuditEvent and the receipt (`change.pr.adopted`) name its head, who opened it, and each commit kardinal did not push with its author. These names are the SCM's free text: they are quoted, without e-mail addresses, cut at 128 bytes, at most 20 commits are listed, and a commit ID that is not hex is recorded as `(invalid)` ([PR evidence](pr-evidence.md)).
