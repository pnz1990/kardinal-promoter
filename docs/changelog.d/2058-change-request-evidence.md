- **Change requests in the PR, the receipt and the evidence pack** (#2058, [page](change-requests.md#in-the-pr-the-receipt-and-the-evidence-pack)). A promotion PR has a `### Change Requests` section with each ServiceNow or Jira ticket of the version in the environment: the key linked to its URL, the state, whether the ticket is the promotion's (and why not), the approvers and when it was read. A custom body gets it with `changeRequestsTable`. The ticketing system's text in it is escaped: HTML, images, links and `@` mentions do not render. A ServiceNow change whose number is not capitals then digits is not read, and a ServiceNow `instanceURL` or Jira `baseURL` with a query or fragment is refused (a ServiceNow one with a path too), because ticket URLs are built from them. Receipts record the tickets as the step read them when it started (`kardinalPromotion.changeRequests`, from the new `status.decision.changeRequests` of the PromotionStep), and each approval's structured fields. `kardinal audit evidence-pack` writes `changerequests.ndjson` (the instances of the period's versions) and `changerequest-templates.ndjson`, and the change management controls name them. **Upgrade note:** apply the updated CRDs (`helm upgrade`, or `config/crd/bases`). The chart's `<release>-auditor` role now reads `changerequests`; with an older chart's role, the pack lists ChangeRequest in `coverage.forbidden`.
