- **Verify gates: agents as verification gates** (#1844, [page](agentic/verify-with-agents.md)) — a PolicyGate with `spec.verify` holds an environment until verifiers found the version to pass a check in an upstream environment: `required` passing votes, `maxFailures` tolerated, `onInconclusive` (`hold`, `retry` or `fail`), a `timeout` from the version's health check there with `onTimeout` (`fail` or `allow`). A verifier writes a **Verdict** (new CRD): `pass`, `fail` or `inconclusive`, a summary, an evidence link and findings, in its own name and with its actor stamp, which the new `<release>-verdicts` admission policy checks. Agents count only when the gate lists them in `allowedAgents`, and never on their own party's work: the version's author agent, its sponsor and the sponsor's other agents do not count. The reason line says where the gate stands (`waiting for verdicts: 1 of 2 (qa-explorer)`), `verdicts.*` exposes the counts to the expression, `status.verdicts` records each verdict and why it does or does not count, and each writes a `VerdictRecorded` or `VerdictRevoked` AuditEvent
- **AgentRuns** — `verify.run` runs an admin-registered agent image (a cluster-scoped **StepTemplate**, new CRD) in the cluster after the verified environment is Verified, `count` times, each attempt as an **AgentRun** (new CRD) that owns a Job and a NetworkPolicy. A run starts only when its StepTemplate's agent (an AgentIdentity) is ready, not suspended and in scope, and its Secrets are labelled for the template. It runs as a ServiceAccount kardinal creates for it (`kr-<run>`), bound only to the chart's new `<release>-agentrun-writer` (create Verdicts) and `<release>-agentrun-reader` (read the namespace under test) roles, and deleted with its bindings when the run ends; its Pod is restricted, with a short-lived token, no git credentials, the run's context in `KARDINAL_*` variables and `/kardinal/context.json`, and egress only to DNS, the API server, the namespace under test and the template's `egress`. The Pod writes one Verdict bound to its run: admission admits it only from that run's ServiceAccount and Pod (exact names), and that ServiceAccount may write nothing else; the Graph takes the run's agent, sponsor and index from the AgentRun. Retries of inconclusive runs are admitted by the Graph. `--step-template-require-digest` refuses images without a digest
- **`kardinal verify`** — record a verdict on the version that runs in an environment (`--pass`, `--fail`, `--inconclusive`, `--summary`, `--evidence`, `--finding`), see which gates count it, `--revoke` it, and `kardinal verify list` the verdicts ([reference](reference/cli/kardinal-verify.md)). The chart adds a `<release>-verifier` ClusterRole (read, create and revoke Verdicts); `<release>-approver` may verify too
- **Verification evidence** — promotion PRs gain a `### Verification` section per verify gate (and the `verificationTable` template function), the UI's stage drawer shows each verify gate's quorum and verdicts, and `kardinal_verdicts_total` and `kardinal_agentruns_total` count verdicts and runs
