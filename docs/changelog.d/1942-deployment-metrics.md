section: Fixed
---

- **Lead time starts at the commit, and the automatic rollback rate counts automatic rollbacks** (#1942) — the Pipeline's p50 and p90 lead time and a version's `commitToProductionMinutes` started when the version was created, so they left out the CI build: a commit made 4 minutes before it reached prod was reported as 1. They now start at `spec.provenance.commitTime` when CI sets it (`kardinal create bundle --commit-time "$(git show -s --format=%cI)"`, or `provenance.commitTime` in the [Bundle API](ci-integration.md#provenance)), and at the version's creation otherwise (a zero or future commit time is refused, one after the version's creation ignored); `deploymentMetrics.leadTimeFromCommit` and `kardinal metrics` say which. A rollback always starts at its own creation. `autoRollbackRateMillis` (`auto_rollback_rate`) counted every rollback, also `kardinal rollback`: it now counts the rollbacks the controller started (`onHealthFailure: rollback`, a RollbackPolicy), and the new `rollbackRateMillis` (`rollback_rate`) counts all of them.
- **Change failure rate and time to restore count retired versions** (#1944) — once a version's Graph was retired, its steps were rebuilt from `status.retiredSteps` without the time its change reached the environment, so it no longer counted as a deployment: in steady state `deploymentMetrics.deployments` stayed at 1 and the change failure rate and time to restore described only the version in flight. Retired records now keep `deployedAt` and `noChanges`, and the metrics count them ([Change failure rate and time to restore](guides/monitoring.md#change-failure-rate-and-time-to-restore)). Records written by an earlier release count when their health check had started, from their step's creation.
