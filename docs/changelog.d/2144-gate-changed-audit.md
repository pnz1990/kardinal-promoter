- **A `GateChanged` AuditEvent when a gate changes under a version in flight** (#2144) — when a PolicyGate that applies to a promoting version is created, edited or deleted and the version's gates get the change, kardinal records it once per version and rebuild: `spec.gates` lists up to 20 changed templates with the old and new digest and generation of the version's gate copies and the field manager the last writer declared (not verified), and `spec.gatesOmitted` counts the rest, so a bulk policy sync cannot crowd other audit records out. When the audit outbox of 32 is full during an AuditEvent write outage, the version's unwritten `GateChanged` records are merged into one before any record is dropped: `spec.mergedRecords` says how many it replaces, and `kardinal_audit_events_coalesced_total` counts the merges ([Audit outbox](guides/security.md#audit-outbox), #2160). A gate moved onto other environments of the version by an edit of its `kardinal.io/applies-to` label alone is recorded too, with `environmentsAdded` and `environmentsRemoved` (#2338). At the upgrade, the gate copies of versions built before this release take the current templates once, and what that changes is recorded as a `GateChanged` that says it happened on upgrade (#2271, #2338). Receipts and compliance evidence can show which rule held a version, and when it changed ([Events written automatically](guides/security.md#events-written-automatically)).
