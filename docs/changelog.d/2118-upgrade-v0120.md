- **The `upgrade-v0120` live suite** (#2118) — runs the steps of [Upgrading from v0.12.0](upgrade.md#upgrading-from-v0120) as written, on Kubernetes 1.35 and 1.37, while versions are in flight. Versions keep their Graphs, steps and PRs and finish; gates that hold a version keep holding it (approval, verify and freeze gates, and a gate edited after the upgrade, which then reaches the version); an AgentRun running at the upgrade finishes and counts; the PromotionReceipt and ChangeRequest kinds are installed and stay inert until you use them; a rollback after the upgrade of a version pinned by digest changes its target environment alone.
