    Use the UI¶

    The controller serves a web UI on port 8082. Open it with a port-forward:

    kubectl port-forward svc/kardinal-promoter -n kardinal-system 8082:8082

    and browse to http://localhost:8082/ui/. To serve it to other clients (an Ingress, a NodePort), turn on a UI auth mode first: see Accessing the UI. To serve it under a path prefix on a shared host, set ui.basePath (Under a path prefix).

    With ui.auth.tokenReview on, the UI reads and acts with your Kubernetes identity, so it shows and changes what your RBAC allows, as kubectl does. A static token (ui.auth.tokenSecretRef, the controller's --ui-auth-token) is one token shared by everyone who has it, with no per-user RBAC.

    Where you are: routes and links¶

    Every screen has its own address, so you can bookmark it, paste it into a ticket, and go Back and Forward through it. The breadcrumb at the top shows Pipelines › namespace › pipeline › environment or version; Pipelines and, below it, the pipeline are links.

    Address Opens
    #/ Pipelines: every pipeline you can see
    #/p/<namespace>/<pipeline> A pipeline's map
    #/p/<namespace>/<pipeline>/env/<environment> The same, with that environment's details open
    #/p/<namespace>/<pipeline>/v/<version> The same, showing that version (a Bundle name)
    #/approvals Approvals: every approval waiting on you, oldest first, with Approve and Decline

    With an environment open, ?v=<version> gives the version shown.

    And after a ?:

    Parameter Does
    tab=versions, tab=activity, tab=metrics Opens that tab of the pipeline page (no tab: the map)
    as=table, as=matrix Shows the Map tab as a table or a matrix instead of the station map
    compare=<version> Draws a second version beside the one shown, and opens the comparison
    node=<gate> Opens a gate's details
    section=why, actions, evidence, history Scrolls an environment's details to that section
    view=ops The operations table instead of the Pipelines board
    filter=…, q=… The Pipelines filter and search

    For example, #/p/payments/checkout/env/prod?section=why opens prod of checkout at the reason it is waiting.

    Pipelines¶

    The start page shows each pipeline as a line of stations, one per environment in promotion order. Each station names the version it runs and when it was Verified. A pipeline that needs a person (a failed promotion, a blocking gate, a hold) says so on its line, and the filter at the top narrows the board to those. ⊞ Ops in the sidebar (or Show the operations table in the palette) lists the same pipelines as a sortable table.

    Freezes¶

    While a freeze holds promotions, every view starts with its banner: whom it holds, where, who froze, since and until when, and why, with Unfreeze or the kardinal unfreeze command. A station the freeze holds, on the map and on the fleet board, is marked frozen and says "Frozen by ".

    Who shipped a version¶

    Every view names who shipped a version the same way, from its verified actor (Actors): a person by name, an agent by name with its sponsor ("fixer for alice"), a bot (a ServiceAccount) as namespace/name, and the controller as kardinal. A glyph says the kind, and the words say it too. What an agent's client asserted about itself, such as its model, is in the title, labelled as asserted. You see it in the stage drawer (Shipped by, and the history), in approvals (who decided), on the version strip, on the fleet board's stations and in the operations table. A version an agent shipped is marked · agent on the map and with the agent glyph on the fleet board. When an agent shipped what a pipeline runs or what is on its way, the filter bar shows Agents, which keeps those pipelines (filter=agents). A version shipped before actor stamps existed names nobody.

    The pipeline page¶

    The top line names the pipeline, its state and where its newest version is, with Pause and Ship a version. Below it are four tabs: Map, Versions, Activity and Metrics.

    Map¶

    The map draws the pipeline as a station map:

    • Stations are the environments. Each shows the version it runs and, under it, the version on its way, with its state in words (Live, Checking health, Waiting for PR merge, Waiting for approval at gate, ...; the labels). The Legend under the map says what each mark and colour means.
    • The thread is the version you follow: one red line through every environment it has reached. Where it stops, something holds it.
    • Gates sit on the edge into the environment they guard. A blocking gate is a bar across the edge, and an approval gate counts its approvals. Select a gate for its expression and its last result.
    • Waves and fleets. Five or more environments at the same step of the order are one band, and a fleet is one platform. Each counts its environments by state; expand it to a grid, a row per state.
    • Needs attention. A strip above the map counts what needs a look: failed environments, environments blocked by each gate, held and frozen environments, and environments that wait. Select an item to go to it.
    • Search matches environment and target names, versions, version (Bundle) names, gate names and gate expressions; Enter and Shift+Enter move through the matches.

    • Zoom. The map has three levels:

      • Detail: every station with its version, what is on its way and its state, and Promote, Roll back and Release hold under the station in focus.
      • Map: names, and a version only where it differs from the environment before.
      • Overview: the shape, with names only on the stations the thread passes, the one in focus, and those that failed or are held.

      It opens at Detail for up to 8 environments, at Map up to 40, and at Overview above that. On a narrow map (below 720 pixels) that does not fit at its level, it opens a level lower. A label that would overlap a more important one is hidden until you point at or focus its station. Drag to pan; zoom with the wheel, a pinch, or the level buttons. A minimap in the corner shows where you are on a large map (it hides while part of the map is under it); select a point to move there.

    Compare two versions. On the version rail above the map, select one version and Shift-select a second, then Compare: the second version's route is drawn as a dashed ink thread, and the comparison lists what differs (images, environments, provenance).

    An environment's details¶

    Select a station (or press Enter on it) to open its details, beside the map on a wide screen and as a sheet over it on a narrow one:

    • Why it's waiting (Why it failed for a failed environment) comes first: the gate that blocks and its reason, the approvals still needed, the verdicts a verify gate waits for, the pull request to merge, the health check in progress. When nothing waits, this is the environment's status.

    • Actions for that environment. Each one asks before it acts, and the button, the question and the result use the same verb:

      Action Asks for
      Approve, Decline an optional comment
      Override gate the gate, how long, and a reason
      Promote a confirmation
      Roll back the version to roll back to, and a reason when you hold the environment on it
      Release hold a confirmation
      Pause pipeline, Resume pipeline a confirmation
      Reject version a reason
      Pause fleet, Resume fleet, Roll back fleet a confirmation (a fleet or one of its targets)

      Open pull request is a link to the step's pull request.

    • Evidence: the images of the version on its way, the steps the promotion ran with their durations, its bake time and its gates.
    • History: the promotion's Kubernetes events in this environment.

    Versions¶

    Every version of the pipeline, newest first, with its state and commit. Select one to show it on the map.

    Activity¶

    What went wrong and what holds: failed promotions grouped across environments (select one to open its environment on the map), and the gates of the version shown, with their conditions and approvals.

    Verify gates¶

    A verify gate shows its quorum, one pip per passing verdict it needs, in Why it's waiting and on Activity. Under it is every verdict: who wrote it, with the same badge as Who shipped a version (an agent with its sponsor), its result in words (Pass, Fail, Inconclusive), its run and age, its findings and how many are high, whether it counts and why not, and a link to its evidence. An inconclusive verdict says what the gate does with it: it holds (onInconclusive: hold), retries the run (retry) or counts it as a fail (fail), and the quorum line counts the inconclusive verdicts that wait. While the gate waits, the kardinal verify command to record a verdict is there to copy.

    Metrics¶

    Release metrics over the last versions: the time to reach the final environments, the deploys that reached them and the rollback rate, and the change failure rate and time to restore from the pipeline's DORA metrics (Monitor).

    The palette and keyboard¶

    Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K) anywhere to open the palette. Type to filter; ↑ and ↓ move, Enter runs, Esc closes.

    • Go to: Pipelines, Approvals, any pipeline, any pipeline › environment, and the versions of the pipeline you are on.
    • Do: approve a version that waits on you, switch the theme (System, Light, Dark), switch the density (comfortable or compact), refresh now, show the operations table or the board, and show the keyboard shortcuts.

    The palette never changes a promotion by itself: Approve, Promote, Roll back and Pause keep their own confirmation.

    Key Anywhere
    ⌘K, Ctrl+K Open the palette
    / Focus the pipeline search
    ? Show or hide the shortcuts
    r Refresh now
    j, k Next or previous environment; on Approvals, next or previous approval
    a Approve: the open environment, the approval in focus, or the only one waiting
    s Ship a version of the open pipeline
    [, ] Follow an older or a newer version
    f Follow the active version
    Esc Close the open panel
    Key On the map
    ← → ↑ ↓ Move between stations (and through an expanded group's grid)
    Home, End The first or the last station
    Enter Open the environment's details (on a group: expand or collapse it)
    Space Expand or collapse the group
    e Expand every group that has something failed, blocked or held
    Shift+E Collapse every group
    +, - Zoom in, out
    0 Fit the map
    / Focus the map search (not the pipeline search)
    Esc Clear the search; else close the open popover; else fit the map. The next Esc closes the environment's details

    Shortcuts do nothing while you type in a field or while a dialog is open.

    On a narrow screen¶

    Below 960 pixels the pipeline list moves behind the menu button at the top left, and an environment's details open as a sheet over the map below 1280 pixels. The map keeps its zoom and search; pinch to zoom.

    The map's toolbar follows the map's own width, not the window's. On a map narrower than 720 pixels (a phone, or the stage drawer open beside it) the attention items get a row of their own, each on one line: a long one is cut with an ellipsis, and pointing at it shows the full text. Search and the View and Zoom controls follow on the next row. The Zoom levels give way to Zoom out, Zoom in and Fit the map buttons.

    Themes and density¶

    The theme follows your system until you pick Light or Dark (the theme button or the palette), and the choice is kept in this browser. Compact density fits more on the screen.
