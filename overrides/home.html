Kubernetes promotion controller

GitOps Governance for the Agentic Era

Agents, CI and people ship changes the same way: kardinal promotes each version through your environments by writing to Git, and enforces governance where the change lands, in the cluster, not in the agent.

  • A verified actor and sponsor. Every version, approval and override records who acted, checked by the API server; a registered agent is recorded with the person who answers for it, and excludeAuthor keeps a version's creator from approving it.
  • A local MCP server. kardinal mcp runs with the agent's own kubeconfig, read-only unless started with --allow-write, and answers a policy pre-flight before the agent acts.
  • Agents as governed verifiers. A verify gate holds an environment until enough eligible agents, people or CI record passing verdicts on the version, never from the author agent's own party, with the evidence in the pull request.
